- The idea of restricting the opponent
	- The goal being to limit the options the opponent has

- Cradle spoke of various combat styles based on Path
	- Pressure from offence for the black dragons
		- Overwhelm the opponent and give them no room to breath
		- Push push and keep pushing until something breaks
		- If you lose momentum you have lost your biggest advantage
	- Adaptability from the gold dragons
		- Being flexible, redirecting force
		- Removes the need for overwhelming momentum 
		- Bend around attacks and capitalize on openings from overextension

			- But only slightly more than others
			- reacting does limit the possibilities but most don't use all possible attacks
			- they have a style which can be predicted
